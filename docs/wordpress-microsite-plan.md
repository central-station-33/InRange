## Content plan (ApartmentSnob-style niche landing page)

**Positioning:** the go-to source for corporate relocation rentals in NJ —
landlords get placement with vetted relocating tenants, tenants get a
curated list instead of scrolling Zillow.

**Page structure:**
1. Hero — "NJ Corporate Relocation Rentals" + one-line value prop + two CTAs
   (Landlords: "List your unit" / Tenants: "See available units")
2. For landlords — why list here (screened corporate tenants, no vacancy
   gaps, agent handles showings) → feeds directly into the
   `rental_landlord` outreach segment as the intake form
3. For tenants — corporate relocation package explainer, furnished vs.
   unfurnished, lease flexibility
4. Featured listings (placeholder until first units are captured through
   the outreach pipeline)
5. Trust signals — J.E.T. Group / JET Realty Advisors affiliation, NJ
   licensing
6. Contact / lead form → should route into the same `ingest-rentals`
   pipeline (landlord side) or a standard buyer-lead form (tenant side)
