# Media Production Outsourcing — Scope of Work Draft

## Being direct about what this is

Hiring someone is not something I can execute — there's no interview,
reference check, or contract-signing I can do on your behalf. What follows
is a draft scope of work / job posting you can post, edit, or hand to a
staffing contact. Treat it as a starting draft, not a finished posting.

---

### Role: Freelance Real Estate Photographer/Videographer (Retainer)

**Engagement type:** Part-time retainer, not an employee — paid per-shoot
or monthly retainer against a shoot-count minimum (pick one; retainer
gives you predictable cost and priority scheduling, per-shoot gives you
zero commitment but no priority when demand spikes).

**Coverage area:** NY/NJ metro — wherever the active listing markets are
(matches the NYC/NJ footprint already in this pipeline).

**Scope:**
- Interior/exterior photography for active listings (standard MLS photo
  package: 20-30 edited photos per property)
- Basic video walkthrough (60-90 sec) for Tier 1-2 listings and the new
  rental-landlord segment specifically — self-managing landlords
  competing with agent-represented listings need better photos than a
  phone snapshot to convert
  a listing lead into a signed unit
- Optional: drone exterior shots where permitted, floor plan sketch

**Turnaround:** 48 hours from shoot to delivered, edited assets — this is
the actual SLA that matters; a great photographer with a 2-week turnaround
doesn't close the gap this item is meant to close

**Rate structure (fill in against your budget):**
- Per-shoot flat rate: $______ (typical range for this scope in NY/NJ
  metro markets is a wide band depending on square footage and whether
  video/drone is included — get 3 quotes rather than anchoring on one
  number)
- OR monthly retainer: $______ for up to ___ shoots/month, overage at
  $______/shoot

**How to find candidates:**
- Local real estate photography networks (most metro markets have 2-3
  photographers who specialize in MLS/listing work and already know the
  compliance requirements)
- Upwork/Thumbtack for a faster first hire, with a paid trial shoot before
  committing to a retainer
- Ask other agents/brokerages in the JET Realty Advisors network for a
  referral — a photographer already used to real estate turnaround
  expectations is worth more than a generalist
